How much each state budgets for health, per person

States budgeted an average of ₹2,774 per person for health in 2025-26. Goa leads at ₹12,736; Bihar trails at ₹1,464, a ninefold gap. Karnataka sits at ₹2,723, 23rd of 31.

Health budget = Medical & Public Health + Family Welfare (RBI State Finances, Statement 37, Jan 2026). Spend per person uses 2025 projected population (MoHFW Technical Group projections). Centre's PM-JAY share is the formal cost-sharing ratio: 60:40 for most states, 90:10 for the North-East, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand and Jammu & Kashmir. The Centre pays that share only for its own eligible families and up to a premium ceiling, so states that cover more families pay far more. Odisha (Jan 2025), Delhi (2025) and West Bengal (Jun 2026) joined PM-JAY recently. Grey areas are union territories with no state budget in RBI's data. Boundaries are indicative. Sources: RBI, population, funding rules, Kerala RTI, West Bengal.
